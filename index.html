About

Ridgeline Digital is a U.S.-based LLC founded by Seth Permann. The company builds and maintains a portfolio of consumer iOS applications and supporting infrastructure. Our products are designed around the principles of functional excellence, user-centric design, and clear, honest communication with the people who use them.

We work primarily in Swift and SwiftUI on Apple platforms, with Python-based backend services where useful. We are intentionally a small, focused operation — built to ship reliable software rather than chase scale for its own sake.
